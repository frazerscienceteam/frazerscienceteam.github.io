Frazer Science Team

Founded by Marc Moody in 2016, The Frazer Science Team (FST) is an organization based in Gainesville, Florida dedicated to nurturing an enthusiasm for science among elementary, middle, and high school students in our local and regional community, aiming to completely eliminate barriers in STEM imposed by gender, socioeconomic status, background, or identity.

We've experienced a rich history and a strong reputation across our 10 years of existence, collecting trophies and individual accolades across the regional, state, and national level in Science Olympiad and Science Bowl. Our work extends beyond the competition scene, as the FST has either hosted or directly assisted in regional and statewide competitions for the aforementioned competitions.

Our team consists of a head coach and two assistant coaches, and is run on the student level by a 5-person captain team. Placement onto our Science Olympiad and Science Bowl teams is done through a selective and data-driven tryout process.

If you have any questions or concerns about the FST, please contact us at frazerscienceteam@gmail.com.
